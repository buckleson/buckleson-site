Getting started

Hyper 0X is a permissioned network. Its validators (the parties that agree on the order of transactions) are named institutions. Every customer runs on their own L2, a separate chain that settles to the Hyper 0X L1. So the first step is a short engagement with our team, not a public sign-up.

1. Tell us about your workload

Section titled “1. Tell us about your workload”

Contact us with what you want to put on-chain: tokenized assets, payments, an exchange, internal wallets or AI-agent security. We will help you decide what you need: your own L2, a Wallet SDK integration, Pavii, or a combination. Pavii is our security gateway for AI agents.

2. Start with a pilot

Section titled “2. Start with a pilot”

We scope a pilot around one use case. Before the pilot starts, we agree on the success metrics with you. We assign a forward-deployed engineer to work inside your stack.

3. Design your L2

Section titled “3. Design your L2”

Together we define the policies of your L2: limits, know-your-customer (KYC) checks and approvals. We also define its privacy model and its block parameters. Then we define the bridges from your L2 to your existing systems and to public chains. A bridge is a link that moves assets between chains.

4. Go to production

Section titled “4. Go to production”

When the pilot meets its metrics, we scale it to a production-grade rollout. Your L2 then settles to the Hyper 0X L1.
