(04) Why Hyper 0X

Banks cannot control a public chain.So we built our own.

On a public chain, you pay its fees and follow its roadmap. When that chain stops, your application stops too. On Hyper 0X, you set the rules for your own L2.

    • Hyper 0X
    • L1 + private L2s

    01 / 03

    How our blockchain works

    Hyper 0X is one L1, the main chain that settles every transaction. Each L2 has its own privacy and size. Add, change or remove an L2 at any time. Blocks grow when traffic is high and shrink when it is low.

    1 pass

    to final, with no rewritten blocks

    Product details
    • Pavii
    • AI-agent security

    02 / 03

    How we protect AI agents

    Pavii checks every prompt and tool call at six gates: injection, privacy, supply chain, budget, egress (outbound data) and output. It lets safe calls through and stops malicious ones. Each decision gets a receipt on Hyper 0X.

    6 gates

    on every agent call

    Product details
    • Encryption
    • Post-quantum-resistant

    03 / 03

    Four layers of encryption

    Every connection uses a hybrid X25519 and ML-KEM-768 key exchange. Each party has its own encrypted record. The master keys stay in an HSM, a hardware device that protects keys.

    4 layers

    in transit, in use, on the ledger, at rest

What does Buckleson do?

Buckleson is a blockchain infrastructure company. We built our own blockchain, Hyper 0X. On it, we deliver custom blockchains, asset tokenization, crypto wallets and exchanges, and security for AI agents.

Can you build a custom blockchain for my company?

Yes, that is our core service. We set up a private L2 on Hyper 0X with your own rules, privacy and bridges. We connect it to your systems and support you from the pilot to production.

How does Hyper 0X keep each customer's data private?

Every customer runs on its own L2, so its transactions never mix with the transactions of others. Data stays encrypted in transit, in use, on the ledger and at rest. Only the parties to a record hold the keys to read it.

Will Pavii block the tool calls my agents need?

No. Pavii stops only a call that breaks a rule. Examples are an injected instruction, personal data that leaves, an unknown package, an empty budget or a destination that is not allowed. Allowed calls go through, and every decision gets a receipt.

Why is a blockchain more secure for my business?

Nobody can secretly edit or delete records on Hyper 0X. Every connection is post-quantum-resistant. Compliance rules run before a transaction executes. So auditors and regulators get one record that they can check.

Who do you work with?

We work with banks and fintechs, asset issuers and AI or enterprise security teams. Most start with one use case, such as a payment flow, a tokenized asset or a group of AI agents. Then they grow from there.

How do we start working with you?

Book a 30-minute call. We learn what you want to put on a blockchain. Then we propose a pilot on your own L2, with a clear scope and clear success measures.

When do your products launch?

Hyper 0X, Pavii, Hyper Wallet, H0X Pay and HyperRun are under development and coming soon. Book a call to learn more.
